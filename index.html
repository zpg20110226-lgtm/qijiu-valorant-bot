Qijiu Valorant Discord Bot

Qijiu Valorant Discord Bot is a Discord bot designed to provide VALORANT players with a convenient way to view their match statistics.

Features

  • Display personal VALORANT match statistics after a match.
  • Provide a short post-match summary including kills, deaths, assists, combat score, and match placement.
  • Provide an optional voice summary in a Discord voice channel.
  • Use player authorization before accessing personal VALORANT data.

Data & Authorization

The product is intended to use Riot Games APIs and Riot Sign On (RSO) where required. Players authorize access to their own account data before personal statistics are retrieved.

Purpose

The purpose of the bot is to make personal match statistics easier and more entertaining to review in a Discord community.
